Rewire token acquisition from PKCE refresh to daemon-sourced Keymaster Mercury tokens (--get-token subprocess), and teach the ZeroConf pairing engine to create accounts — the two engine pieces that make ZeroConf pairing the ONE auth step.

Output: TokenManager and Credentials fully PKCE-free at runtime; Client.pm works unchanged.
</objective>
