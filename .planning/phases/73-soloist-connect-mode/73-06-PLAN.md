<objective>
Close the fake-libpulse portion of UAT gap 3 (Spotify→LMS skip: long audible delay,
Spotify progress stuck at 0/1s while LMS shows correct progress + metadata).

Purpose: the D-04 audio transport currently ignores the sink-flush request Soloist
issues when it discards buffered audio, so stale prior-track PCM keeps draining and
keeps inflating the reported sink latency.
Output: pa_stream_flush implemented as a real ring flush, pinned by the host test.

## Diagnosis evidence (UAT session log + code/binary inspection this planning session)

- The UAT hypothesis of a command-echo loop is DISPROVEN: after the app-side skips
  in the 08:42-08:52 log window, Connect.pm sent NO commands back to Soloist (the
  change handler is metadata-only on the observed path, its conditional force-play
  did not fire, and the existing source-marking suppressed all internal echoes).
  Connect.pm therefore needs no change for this gap.
- `pa_stream_flush` in fake-libpulse.c (line ~1154) only invokes the success
  callback — the ring is untouched. RING_CAPACITY is 705600 bytes = exactly 4.0s
  of 44100Hz/2ch/S16 audio.
- `_stream_refresh_timing` reports read_index = bytes_written - ring fill. With a
  full ring, Soloist sees ~4s of sink latency; its position math (decode position
  minus sink latency) therefore reports ~0 for the first seconds of every new
  track — the "Spotify progress stuck at 0/1s" symptom — while the stale 4s of the
  PREVIOUS track drains audibly (the "lange Verzögerung").
- The soloist binary imports pa_stream_flush (confirmed via strings on the pinned
  1.3.7.489 binary), i.e. it does request a sink flush when discarding buffered
  audio; the stub just drops the request today.
- Residual after this fix: squeezelite's own output buffer (~2-3s on the dev
  player, more on Radio/Touch hardware) still drains old PCM — that portion is the
  pre-existing, documented behavior of the continuous-stream Connect model (known
  error: connect-track-delay-hardware, identical on the librespot path) and is out
  of scope here.
</objective>
