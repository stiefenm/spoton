<objective>
Mitigate RC-4 of the Soloist Browse audio stutter (debug session `.planning/debug/soloist-browse-stutter.md`, Resolution section): Soloist's internal decoder/worker threads take 10-215s+ to start producing audio after a play command (five fake-libpulse-side mechanisms tested and refuted across two debug sessions — this latency is not fixable from the PA-API surface). LMS's `StreamingController::_RetryOrNext` reconnects after ~10s of premature stream end, resetting position to 0 on every retry — far less patient than Soloist's worst case, so each retry gets a small burst crumb and the cycle repeats indefinitely ("progressive stutter, ~5s silence, repeats cyclically").

D-17's chosen lever: a stream-handoff gate. `getNextTrack` (ProtocolHandler.pm §640-802) is LMS's designed async point BEFORE the stream/transcode pipeline opens — deferring `$successCb->()` there means squeezelite is never handed the /stream URL (neither via canDirectStream §213-275 nor via new()'s proxy substitution §532-639) until Soloist demonstrably produces audio. The stall window becomes visible-as-buffering instead of audible-as-stutter, and the LMS retry loop never arms because no stream has started and "ended prematurely".

Readiness must be two-stage, per the live evidence:
- A bare `playback_changed 'playing'` is NOT sufficient — the captures show 'playing' flipping to 'paused' (self-cork) within ~20ms of the initial burst (evidence entry 20:28: playing 23.244 → paused 23.264, real audio only at 33.575).
- Stale `position_sync` frames from a previous track could arrive between our play command and Soloist's internal switch.

Therefore: Stage A = `track_changed` confirming the expected URI (the D-15 equality branch or the seeded-match branch from Plan 76-09 — this is why 76-09 is a hard dependency). Stage B = after Stage A, the first `position_sync` with `speed` != 0 OR a `playback_changed 'playing'` that survives a short confirm window without an immediately following 'paused'. Timeout fail-open.

Purpose: convert the unrecoverable stutter loop into a bounded, clean delayed start, without touching LMS core, Connect, or librespot.

Output: `waitForBrowseReady` machinery in SoloistWS.pm, gated successCb in ProtocolHandler.pm, unit tests, UAT checklist scenario, CHANGELOG entries for plans 76-09..76-11.
</objective>
